Answers

Frequently asked questions

How much does a typical website cost?

A clean single-page site for a local clinic, shop, or consultancy typically starts around ₹8,000 to ₹12,000. Multi-page business sites or tools with custom automation depend on the scope. Send me a quick WhatsApp note with what you need and I'll give you a clear, fixed estimate.

Do I need to arrange hosting and domain myself?

You can purchase it yourself, or I can walk you through purchasing it on standard platforms (like Hostinger or Namecheap). In either case, the billing and credentials remain 100% under your ownership.

Can the website be in Marathi?

Yes. Many local businesses benefit greatly from conversational Marathi (बोलीभाषा), where standard terms like Website, WhatsApp, Mobile, and Timing remain in English while the rest is in natural Marathi. Bilingual toggles are also supported.

How do customers pay through the website?

We can integrate direct UPI payment links (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) for instant fee transfers, or formal payment gateways like Cashfree/Razorpay if you need automated receipts and card support.

What happens after the website is launched?

Every project includes 30 days of complimentary support for minor text updates, photo changes, or question solving. Even after 30 days, I am always reachable directly on WhatsApp.
